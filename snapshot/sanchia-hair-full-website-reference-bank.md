# 5. Root Smudge & Toner

## Toner

A toner is used after lightening or colouring the hair to refine, balance and perfect the final shade. It helps neutralise unwanted tones, such as brassiness or excess warmth, while enhancing the desired colour result for a softer, more polished finish.

## Root Smudge & Toner

A Root Smudge and Toner service creates a softer, more natural-looking colour result while extending the life of highlights or balayage.

The root smudge gently blends the natural root colour into lighter lengths, softening harsh lines and creating a seamless transition as the hair grows. A toner then refines and perfects the final colour.

Strong lines:

> Beautiful colour isn’t about where the highlights begin. It’s about creating a seamless flow of light and depth that looks as though it belongs naturally to you.

> Great colour should never have a starting point or an ending point — it should simply flow.

## Services to include

- Spiral Perm
- Spot Perming
- Modern Textured Perming

## Key points

- Modern perming can create waves, curls, root lift, body, beachy movement or improved shape.
- The result depends on hair type, condition, length, density and desired finish.
- Consultation is essential.
- Hair should not be washed for 72 hours after perming.

## Spot perming

Spot perming is a bespoke technique where only selected areas are permed. It can support sections where natural curl has weakened due to hormones, pregnancy, ageing, menopause, medication, heat styling or changes in hair condition.

# 14. Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy

## Important correction

Sanchia will **not** put Finnish water directly into the Micro-Mist machine because it may damage the machine. Finnish water may be sprayed into the hair separately as part of a treatment step, if used.

## Direction

Micro-Mist should be explained as advanced hydration and treatment-support technology, without overclaiming.

## Key points

- Micro-Mist creates an ultra-fine vapour/mist.
- It can help professional treatments absorb more effectively.
- Warm mist helps soften/open the cuticle.
- Cooling phase helps smooth/close the cuticle.
- Supports hydration, softness, shine, manageability and scalp comfort.

## Finnish water note

If Finnish water is used, it should be described carefully as a separate treatment spray, not as water placed into the machine.

Suggested safe wording:

> Where suitable, selected treatment steps may include a separate Finnish water misting or spray application before or during the treatment journey. This is used as part of the hair preparation or treatment layering process, not inside the Micro-Mist machine.

## QiQi Hair Controller — replaces Brazilian blow-dry / Nano Keratin-style smoothing

QiQi Hair Controller is now the smoothing and texture-control pathway for Sanchia Hair. It replaces the previous Brazilian blow-dry / Nano Keratin-style wording and should not be grouped with Japanese Head Spa, Micro-Mist or general treatment add-ons.

Positioning:

- a modern alternative to traditional Brazilian blow-dry or keratin-style smoothing,
- flexible texture control rather than one fixed result,
- personalised around the guest’s hair goals, natural texture, history, condition and lifestyle,
- supports reduced frizz, softer curls, smoother waves, improved manageability, shine, reduced bulk and easier styling,
- designed to work with the hair’s personality rather than remove it,
- suitable for many hair types, including coloured, highlighted and chemically treated hair, subject to consultation,
- no long waiting period for suitable guests: hair can usually be washed, exercised with, tied up or styled on the same day,
- results can typically last around four to six months with correct homecare, depending on hair type, condition, lifestyle, washing, heat styling and aftercare.

Client-facing quote:

> Control does not mean losing your natural texture. It means gaining the freedom to wear your hair the way that works best for you.

Alternative quote:

> The best hair is not the smoothest, straightest or curliest. It is the hair that fits effortlessly into your life.

Website note:

Remove or avoid: Nano Keratin, Brazilian blow-dry, Japanese Hair Spa, Micro-Mist and general treatment wording in the QiQi section. QiQi should have its own clear section/page explaining what it is, what it can do, how long it lasts and why Sanchia has chosen it.
