# Sanchia Hair — Step 1 Voice Copy Brief

## Important: Sanchia does not need to resend service information
All service/product/detail information already supplied should be treated as the reference bank. Use the current website files, Notion reference pages, memory, and existing draft/reference markdown files before asking Sanchia for anything again.

## Step 1 goal
Rewrite/audit the whole website voice so it feels like Sanchia speaking directly to the right guest:
- luxury/editorial, but warm and human
- direct and reassuring, not corporate
- personal without becoming too casual
- clear enough for busy City clients who want to understand what to book
- premium enough for clients who value expertise, privacy, health-led care and can afford bespoke work
- honest and trustworthy without needing to say “honest” directly
- educational in a calm, client-friendly way: explain the services and how Sanchia works so guests understand the difference between options and can choose the best direction with confidence
- always bring the language back to bespoke, tailored hair journeys: everything is created around the individual guest, their personality, lifestyle, hair history and long-term goals
- include the idea of creating an individual signature look that feels personal, refined and made especially for them

## Audience
The copy should speak to guests who:
- work in/around the City or value efficient, considered appointments
- can afford bespoke colour, treatment and transformation work
- care about quality, condition, privacy, values and trust
- want expert guidance rather than bargain pricing
- respond to calm, polished, luxury wording with warmth

## Must include across the site
Do not leave these out:
- highlights
- balayage
- colour
- grey blending
- permanent / semi / demi colour
- cutting
- natural curls
- modern perming
- QiQi Hair Controller
- Micro Mist treatments
- Japanese Head Spa
- Korean Head Spa
- Private Studio appointments
- products / homecare
- Organic Colour Systems as the primary colour house
- second professional colour option for particular hair types/histories/results
- cruelty-free values
- vegan-friendly where accurate
- sustainability / responsible product choices
- Green Salon Collective / community / charity where relevant
- Miss Evie / animal values
- Stā Studios dog-friendly note
- gift cards
- Google reviews / Solo reviews
- 7am City appointments

## Voice blend
Use a combination of:
1. Luxury editorial — polished, refined, visually beautiful.
2. Warm directness — feels like Sanchia speaking to the guest.
3. Expert reassurance — guides the client clearly without sounding cold.

Avoid:
- generic salon wording
- over-medical claims
- overpromising results
- sounding too business-like
- sounding too cheap or offer-led
- making the client work hard to understand the service

## What Sanchia needs to provide
Only one thing at this stage: choose whether the sample voice feels right.
She should not need to resend service/product details.

Create one copy-review file with the full rewritten/audited website copy in sections, before making another large layout pass.
