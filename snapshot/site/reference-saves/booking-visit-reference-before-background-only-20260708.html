Important Information

Protective policies, calm and clear.

The policies sit in readable dropdown-style sections so the page does not become one heavy legal wall, but the important protection is still visible and professional.

Booking, prices & consultation forms

Read

All live prices and appointment options sit on Solo. Consultation forms, photos and hair history help Sanchia check the right service, timing and suitability before the appointment is confirmed.

Colour corrections & suitability

Read

Colour corrections, previous colour, home colour, lightening, smoothing, texture services and bigger changes may need extra consultation time before booking. Skin sensitivity testing or strand testing can sit inside this section where it is needed for safety, colour history, condition or result planning.

Deposits / pre-payments

Read

Deposits or pre-payments secure Sanchia’s time, planning and product preparation. The policy should clearly state when deposits are transferable, non-refundable or deducted from the final appointment.

Cancellations / moving appointments

Read

Clear notice periods protect the diary, especially for longer colour, correction, QiQi, Head Spa or treatment appointments. Late changes, repeat moving or short-notice cancellation may affect deposits or future booking access.

Late arrival / no show

Read

Late arrival may shorten, adapt or reschedule the appointment if there is not enough time to complete the service safely and beautifully. Missed appointments or no-shows may lose the appointment payment or deposit.

Refunds / adjustments

Read

Completed services, consultation fees and deposits need clear terms. If a client has a concern, they should contact Sanchia within the stated concern window so the hair can be reviewed before any adjustment is agreed.

Retail & product orders

Read

Retail and professional homecare guidance should be clear. Include product ordering, pre-orders, proof of purchase, faulty items, and whether unused and unopened products can be returned or exchanged.

Gift Card Policy

Read

Gift cards should explain how to buy, how to redeem, expiry dates, what they can be used for, lost card rules, non-refundable terms and no cash exchange. Any promotional gift card terms should be clear before purchase.

Recommend a friend

Read

Referral or recommend-a-friend offers should be explained clearly: who qualifies, how the reward is applied, whether it can be used with other offers, and that terms may change where needed.

Children / dogs / salon comfort

Read

Children, babies or dogs should be agreed before the appointment. The visit must remain calm, safe and suitable for the service being booked, the salon environment and other clients.
