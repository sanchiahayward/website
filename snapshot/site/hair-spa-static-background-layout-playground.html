Micro Mist vs Head Spa

Micro Mist is a focused treatment boost. Japanese and Korean Head Spa are the full private studio rituals.

Click to read more

Micro Mist can be a shorter treatment or added to selected services where suitable. Head Spa appointments are longer, more layered and designed as a complete scalp, hair and wellbeing experience.
