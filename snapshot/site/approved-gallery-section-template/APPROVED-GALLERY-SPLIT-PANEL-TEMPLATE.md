## Visual rules
- Cream editorial writing panel on the left.
- Vertical photo story on the right.
- Cream fades softly into the first photo.
- Text is dark and readable.
- Keep the first photo clear enough to show curl / hair definition.
- No oval glow.
- No hard cream strip across the whole image.
- No green side strip.
- No black overlay.
- No harsh joins between photos.
- Luxury, calm, warm, editorial feeling.
