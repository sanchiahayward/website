Important Information

Protective policies, calm and clear.

The policies sit in readable boxes so the page does not become one heavy legal wall, but the important protection is still visible and professional.

Booking, prices & consultation forms

All live prices and appointment options sit on Solo. Consultation forms, photos and hair history help Sanchia check the right service, timing and suitability.

Colour corrections & suitability

Colour corrections, home colour, lightening, smoothing, texture services and bigger changes may need extra consultation time. Skin or strand testing can sit here where needed.

Deposits / pre-payments

Deposits or pre-payments secure Sanchia’s time, planning and product preparation. Terms should state when deposits are transferable, non-refundable or deducted.

Cancellations / moving appointments

Clear notice periods protect the diary. Late changes, repeat moving or short-notice cancellation may affect deposits or future booking access.

Late arrival / no show

Late arrival may shorten, adapt or reschedule the appointment if there is not enough time. Missed appointments may lose the payment or deposit.

Refunds / adjustments

Completed services, consultation fees and deposits need clear terms. Concerns should be raised within the stated concern window so the hair can be reviewed.

Retail & product orders

Include product ordering, pre-orders, proof of purchase, faulty items, and whether unused and unopened products can be returned or exchanged.

Gift Card Policy

Explain how to buy, redeem, expiry dates, what they can be used for, lost card rules, non-refundable terms and no cash exchange.

Recommend a friend

Referral offers should be clear: who qualifies, how the reward is applied, whether it can be used with other offers, and that terms may change.

Children / dogs / salon comfort

Children, babies or dogs should be agreed before the appointment. The visit must remain calm, safe and suitable for the service and salon environment.
