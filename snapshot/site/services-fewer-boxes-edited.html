The consultation is where the journey begins. Sanchia reviews your hair history, lifestyle, goals, condition and suitability before advising what is realistic. For colour, perming and smoothing treatments, a skin sensitivity test and/or strand test may be needed before an appointment is confirmed. Guests can pop into the salon for a complimentary test where suitable, or request a posted skin sensitivity test if visiting the salon is not practical.

Colour Journeys

Colour options explained clearly: longer-lasting coverage, softer refreshes and glossy tonal changes.

Highlights Balayage Toner Grey blending
Read more

Highlights are never approached as one standard pattern. Sanchia looks at your haircut, natural fall, face shape, colour history, density and maintenance goals before choosing the right technique for your hair. Balayage is a bespoke colour placement technique, not one fixed look. Root smudge and toner services are usually add-ons to colour, highlights or balayage, and are recommended during consultation where they will improve the final result.

A haircut begins with how your hair behaves in real life. Sanchia looks at your natural fall, growth pattern, density, condition, face shape, lifestyle and how much time you want to spend styling at home before shaping the hair. Modern perming and QiQi are used for soft movement, curl support, shape control and easier day-to-day styling where suitable.

Hair Health & Rituals

Head Spa, Micro Mist and treatment support keep the page calm, premium and guided, rather than cluttered with extra cards.

Head Spa and Micro Mist are connected through hair health, scalp care and treatment support, but they are not the same booking. A Japanese or Korean Head Spa is a standalone treatment experience, while Micro Mist is a shorter treatment support service that can be booked separately or used as an add-on.
