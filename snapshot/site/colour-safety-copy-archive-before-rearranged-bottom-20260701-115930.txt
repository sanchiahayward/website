          <div id="colour-template-suitability" class="colour-template-detail">
            <p>Suitability means looking honestly at what your hair can safely do now, not just the colour you like in a picture. Previous colour, lightening history, hair strength, condition and maintenance all affect the safest route.</p>
            <p>Some colour journeys can happen in one appointment. Others are better planned slowly so the hair still feels beautiful, wearable and healthy afterwards.</p>
            <a class="colour-template-close" href="#colour">Close</a>

          <div id="colour-template-testing" class="colour-template-detail">
            <p>A skin sensitivity test checks how your skin responds to the colour product before a colour appointment. If it is needed, you can pop into the salon for a complimentary skin sensitivity test, or request a posted test if visiting is not practical.</p>
            <p>A strand test checks how a small piece of hair responds, helping assess condition, colour history, lift, tone and suitability before committing to a larger colour service.</p>
            <p>A wet hair strand test will include the information you give me next, so this wording can be corrected properly before the page is approved.</p>
