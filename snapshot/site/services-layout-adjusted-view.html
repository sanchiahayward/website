01

Consultation

For new guests, colour, perming, smoothing treatments, major changes or anyone unsure what to book.

Read more

The consultation is where the journey begins. Sanchia reviews your hair history, lifestyle, goals, condition and suitability before advising what is realistic. For colour, perming and smoothing treatments, a skin sensitivity test and/or strand test may be needed before an appointment is confirmed. Guests can pop into the salon for a complimentary test where suitable, or request a posted skin sensitivity test if visiting the salon is not practical. 02

Cutting & Styling

Haircuts, blow dries, shaping and finishing designed around how your hair naturally sits and how you want to feel day to day.

Read more

A haircut begins with how your hair behaves in real life. Sanchia looks at your natural fall, growth pattern, density, condition, face shape, lifestyle and how much time you want to spend styling at home before shaping the hair.

She uses a head-mapping approach to plan the shape: where the hair falls, where weight sits, where volume is needed and how the haircut will grow out. Depending on your hair, the cut may combine wet cutting, dry cutting, freehand refinement and sections clipped away so each area can be shaped with intention.

The aim is to create a cut that feels balanced, wearable and personal — something that supports your hair rather than fighting against it.

“A good haircut should make your hair easier to live with, not harder to manage.”

03

Curl Cutting & Styling

A longer appointment than a standard cut and blow dry, with curl assessment, treatment, styling and freehand refinement.

Read more

This is a more detailed appointment than a standard cut and blow dry because curls need time to be assessed, cleansed, treated, styled and refined in their natural form.

The appointment begins with a personalised consultation, carefully assessing your curl pattern, natural growth pattern, hair history, density, condition, lifestyle and how you prefer to style your hair at home.

Sanchia works with the hair in its natural form, shaping around the way each curl falls so the cut respects movement, balance and individuality. Depending on your hair, the cut may combine wet cutting, dry cutting and freehand refinement, with sections clipped away or refined separately so each area can be shaped with intention.

After cleansing and treating the hair to support condition, your curls are styled in their natural form and the shape is refined again where needed — subtly carving, slicing or adjusting to perfect the silhouette.

“Curly hair should be shaped around the curl, not forced into a standard haircut.”

04

Highlights & Babylights

Bespoke lightening using tailored placement, babylights, depth and soft dimension to enhance your haircut, face shape and natural movement.

Read more

Highlights are never approached as one standard pattern. Sanchia looks at your haircut, natural fall, face shape, colour history, density and maintenance goals before choosing the right technique for your hair.

That may include fine babylights for a soft natural lift, brighter face-framing pieces, colour placement to enhance the haircut, or carefully added depth and lowlights so the colour has movement and dimension rather than looking flat.

The aim is to create brightness, softness, contrast and movement in the right places — so the highlights work with your hair, your cut and your overall style, not just as light pieces placed on top.

A root smudge and/or toner may also be recommended as an additional add-on where needed. This is confirmed during consultation so the colour plan, timing and price are clear before the appointment.

“Highlights should be placed with purpose, not painted from a pattern.”

05

Balayage

Bespoke freehand colour for sun-kissed, lived-in brightness, soft dimension and a lower-maintenance grow-out.

Read more

Balayage is a bespoke colour placement technique, not one fixed look. Sanchia looks at your natural base, colour history, haircut, face shape, density, movement and maintenance goals before deciding where lightness, depth and softness should sit.

The colour may be painted more freely than traditional highlights to create a lived-in result, but the placement is still carefully planned. The aim is to create brightness where it flatters, depth where the hair needs softness or contrast, and a grow-out that feels gentle rather than harsh.

A consultation may be needed before balayage, especially for new guests, big colour changes or corrective work. A root smudge and/or toner may also be recommended as an additional add-on where needed. This is confirmed during consultation so the colour plan, timing and price are clear before the appointment.

“Balayage should look effortless, but the placement should never be accidental.”

06

Root Smudge & Toner

Add-on colour services that soften the root area, refine tone and create a more seamless finish.

Read more

Root smudge and toner services are usually add-ons to colour, highlights or balayage, and are recommended during consultation where they will improve the final result.

A root smudge gently blends your natural root colour into lighter lengths, softening harsh lines and helping the colour grow out more seamlessly. It can make highlights or balayage feel softer, more expensive and more natural-looking.

A toner is used after lightening or colouring to refine, balance and perfect the final shade. It can help neutralise unwanted warmth or brassiness, add softness, enhance brightness, create depth or make the colour feel more polished.

Together, they help extend the life of highlights or balayage and create a softer flow between light, depth and your natural colour.

“Great colour should never have a hard starting point — it should simply flow.”

07

Permanent, Semi & Demi Colour

Colour options explained clearly: longer-lasting coverage, softer refreshes and glossy tonal changes.

Read more

Permanent colour is usually chosen when the goal is stronger coverage, a longer-lasting colour change or more noticeable grey coverage. It tends to grow out rather than simply wash away, so the maintenance plan is discussed during consultation.

Semi-permanent colour is softer and lower commitment. It can refresh tone, add shine, gently enhance your natural colour or soften the feel of the hair without creating the same level of long-term commitment.

Demi-permanent colour sits between the two. It is useful for gloss, depth, tonal change, blending, refreshing faded colour and softer grey blending, usually fading more gradually than permanent colour.

Sanchia works with Organic Colour Systems as her primary colour house because it supports her health-led, condition-conscious approach. Her secondary professional colour system is YUV, chosen where its precise custom formulation, refillable colour system and lower-waste approach are better suited to the hair history, desired result or technical need. Sanchia will explain which colour system she is using and why during consultation.

The right choice depends on your hair history, condition, grey level, skin sensitivity requirements, maintenance wishes and the finish you want: soft and natural, glossy and rich, blended, brightened or more transformative.

08

Grey Blending

Blends grey away softly, works with natural silver and softens harsh regrowth lines.

Read more

Grey blending is for guests who do not necessarily want to cover every grey completely, but want their colour to feel softer, fresher and more intentional as natural silver grows through.

Sanchia looks at your natural grey pattern, base colour, previous colour history, haircut, face shape and maintenance goals before choosing the best route. This may include highlights, lowlights, babylights, demi colour, toners or carefully placed depth to soften the contrast between coloured hair and natural silver.

The aim is to reduce harsh regrowth lines and create a more graceful grow-out, whether you want subtle blending, brighter pieces around the face, a softer transition into natural grey, or a more polished colour result that still feels like you.

Grey blending may take more than one appointment depending on previous colour, condition and the amount of contrast in the hair. A consultation, photos, skin sensitivity testing and strand testing may be needed before the colour plan is confirmed.

Modern Perming

Soft waves, root volume, spot perming and natural curl support using Organic Colour Systems perming products.

Read more

Modern perming is not about making the hair look obviously “permed”. It is about creating soft movement, natural-looking wave, root lift, selected curl support or fuller-looking shape where the hair needs it.

Sanchia uses Organic Colour Systems Think Curl for modern perming services. This curl activator and neutraliser system has been chosen because it sits with her softer, health-led approach to hair. It is enriched with plant amino acids and supported with Keep Curl aftercare, with conditioning ingredients chosen to support definition, curl retention, softness and shine.

Every modern perming service includes Micro Mist treatment support within the price where suitable. Sanchia may also use the Micro Mist machine during the perming process to support moisture, comfort and condition as part of the overall service experience.

Your hair history, condition, natural movement, desired result, strand testing and homecare will all be considered before the final perm plan is confirmed. If you would like to understand the perming process in more detail, the full Modern Perming section further down the website explains the journey, suitability and aftercare.

“A modern perm should look like your hair learned a softer new movement — not like it was forced into one.”

QiQi Hair Controller

Personalised QiQi Hair Controller for frizz, bulk, curl softening, shape control, shine and easier styling.

Read more

QiQi Hair Controller is a flexible hair-control service, chosen as Sanchia’s modern alternative to older smoothing services. It can be used in different ways: to reduce frizz, soften curls or waves, reduce bulk, support shape control, improve shine, support manageability and make the hair easier to style day to day.

The aim is not always to make the hair completely straight. Sanchia can tailor the service to soften, smooth, control or reshape how the hair behaves, depending on your natural movement, hair history, condition, lifestyle, styling routine and the finish you want.

QiQi is part of Sanchia’s transparent product story too. It has been chosen because it supports a more flexible, condition-conscious control approach, and because the product information aligns with values-led choices such as vegan-friendly formulas and products not tested on animals.

Where suitable, results can last around four to six months with the correct homecare and maintenance. Suitability, aftercare, timing and realistic expectations are discussed before booking so the service supports the hair rather than overworking it.

“QiQi should give your hair more control, while still letting it feel like you.”

11

Head Spa / Micro Mist

Standalone Japanese/Korean Head Spa rituals, plus shorter Micro Mist treatment support and add-ons.

Read more

Head Spa and Micro Mist are connected through hair health, scalp care and treatment support, but they are not the same booking.

A Japanese or Korean Head Spa is a standalone treatment experience, designed around scalp comfort, relaxation, cleansing, hydration, massage-style ritual and a more complete hair-and-scalp reset.

Micro Mist is a shorter treatment support service. It can be booked as a focused treatment where suitable, or used as an add-on within selected services such as colour, modern perming or condition-focused hair journeys to help support moisture, softness and manageability.

Sanchia will choose the most suitable route around your scalp, hair condition, service plan and how much support your hair needs. If you would like to understand the difference in more detail, the treatment section further down the website explains the Head Spa and Micro Mist pathways more fully.

“A treatment should feel beautiful in the moment, but it should also support how your hair feels afterwards.”
