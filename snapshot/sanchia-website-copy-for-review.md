## About Sanchia

### Eyebrow
About Sanchia

### Heading
Considered hair, guided by experience.

### Copy
With more than thirty years in the industry, Sanchia creates curated hair journeys built around intention, honesty and the way you live.

Every session is tailored to your individual needs. Sanchia works one-to-one, taking time to understand your hair history, natural texture, condition, lifestyle, routine and long-term goals before designing your colour, texture, cut or treatment journey — with the aim of creating a signature look that feels uniquely yours.

The process is thoughtful and personal: respecting your hair’s growth, integrity and structure, then creating a bespoke plan that supports enduring transformation without compromise — especially for early 7am City guests who want their hair finished before the working day begins.

Colour safety

### Copy
For colour, balayage, highlights, grey blending and colour corrections, the journey should feel expert and reassuring — not rushed.

New colour guests may need an in-person consultation (£35), photos, hair history and a valid skin test before colour is confirmed.

The £35 in-person consultation includes skin testing or hair strand testing where suitable. Guests can also request a posted skin-test kit after completing the form and pre-paying, if appropriate.

If skin-test requirements are not completed in time, your colour appointment may need to be rescheduled.

A restorative hair and scalp ritual using professional treatment products with gentle heat/mist support to help the hair feel softer, smoother and more conditioned.
