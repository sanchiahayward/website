# Website Design Video Notes — Reference for Sanchia Hair

**Video:** https://youtu.be/kEN4pp-UFf0  
**Length:** 1 hour 34 minutes  
**Purpose of this document:** turn the video into practical rules we can use while finishing the Sanchia Hair website.

---

## 1. Main message from the video

A website should not just look pretty.

It must help the right client understand:

1. who you are
2. what you offer
3. why they should trust you
4. what they should do next

The video says many websites fail because they are either:

- pretty but unclear
- full of information but badly structured
- hard to navigate
- not designed around what the visitor needs first

For Sanchia Hair, this means the website must feel luxury and editorial, but it must still be very easy for a client to use.

---

## 2. The biggest rule for Sanchia Hair

Every part of the website must answer this question:

> Does this help the right client take the right next step?

For Sanchia Hair, the right next steps are usually:

- understand Sanchia’s approach
- choose the right service pathway
- book a consultation
- message on WhatsApp
- trust the salon before booking

If a section does not help one of those things, it should be simplified.

---

## 3. Hero section — first screen

The video says the hero section does most of the heavy lifting.

### What the hero must do

The first screen should quickly show:

- who Sanchia Hair is
- what kind of experience it offers
- why it feels different
- what the client should click next

### What this means for us

The first screen should not be cluttered.

It should have:

- a strong luxury image or slideshow
- calm headline
- short supporting line
- one main booking action
- one softer WhatsApp/message action
- clear 7am City appointment message

### Avoid

- too many buttons
- too many menu links
- too much text
- lots of competing boxes
- anything that makes the client work too hard

---

## 4. Navigation — keep it simple

The video says navigation should give clear paths.

It also says vague labels like “More” are not helpful.

### What this means for Sanchia Hair

The top navigation should stay simple:

- About
- Services
- Booking

This keeps the top clean and luxury.

Detailed services can sit inside the Services section instead of crowding the top bar.

### Services can then guide clients to:

- Colour
- Smoothing / QiQi
- Perms / texture
- Hair Spa / Micro Mist
- Curls
- Consultation

---

## 5. Hierarchy — what should stand out first

The video explains that confusing websites often have broken hierarchy.

Hierarchy means deciding what the eye sees first, second and third.

The three tools are:

1. size
2. contrast
3. spacing

### What this means for us

On the Sanchia Hair website:

- the main message should be biggest
- the 7am City appointment bar must stand out clearly
- booking buttons must be easy to see
- service boxes must not all fight for attention
- images must be in proportion to the copy
- nothing should look squeezed or randomly placed

---

## 6. Spacing and breathing room

The video says when everything competes for attention, nothing gets attention.

Luxury design needs space.

### For Sanchia Hair

We need:

- fewer visible elements at once
- larger calm sections
- better spacing around copy
- images sized in proportion to text
- not too many boxes on one screen

This is especially important on iPad and phone.

---

## 7. Copy — less visible text, more layered information

The video says too much visible information overwhelms visitors.

### For Sanchia Hair

We should use:

- short elegant intro text
- clear section headings
- “Read more” for detail
- service pathways instead of huge walls of text

This lets serious clients read more without overwhelming new visitors.

---

## 8. Social proof builds trust

The video explains that social proof is not just reviews.

It can be:

- testimonials
- review snippets
- logos
- featured-in sections
- client results
- trusted product brands
- years of experience
- strong before/after imagery

### For Sanchia Hair

Useful trust points:

- 30+ years of experience
- Organic Colour Systems
- Green Salon Collective
- Stā Studios near Bank
- Google/Solo reviews
- before and after gallery
- Miss Evie and cruelty-conscious values, where appropriate
- specialist services: curls, perms, QiQi, Hair Spa, colour

These should be placed naturally, not dumped all together.

---

## 9. Buttons and calls to action

The video says calls to action should feel like the natural next step.

The main button should be visually clear.

### For Sanchia Hair

Main actions should be:

- Book consultation
- Message WhatsApp

### Button rules

- one main button should stand out more
- second button can be softer
- buttons should be placed where the client is ready to act
- every section should guide the client somewhere useful

---

## 10. Mobile and iPad

The video says mobile is not just desktop made smaller.

Mobile must be designed as its own experience.

### For Sanchia Hair

Phone and iPad must have:

- large enough text
- large enough buttons
- no cramped boxes
- no tiny navigation
- no hidden important message
- no text running too wide
- images stacked clearly
- enough breathing room

The 7am City appointment bar must remain visible and readable.

---

## 11. Animation and movement

The video says animation should help the visitor, not show off.

### For Sanchia Hair

Use only soft movement:

- gentle slideshow
- subtle button hover
- soft fade-in if needed

Avoid:

- flying elements
- complicated scroll effects
- anything that slows the client down
- anything that feels like a tech demo

Luxury should feel calm, not busy.

---

## 12. Accessibility and dyslexia-friendly site design

The video says accessibility helps everyone.

### For Sanchia Hair

The website should have:

- readable font sizes
- good contrast
- clear headings
- simple navigation
- short paragraphs
- obvious buttons
- mobile-friendly spacing

This also supports Sanchia’s own need for clearer visual structure.

---

## 13. What this means for our current website work

### Keep

- luxury organic editorial direction
- soft green / cream palette
- slideshow box idea
- simple top navigation
- 7am City appointment message
- curated hair journey wording
- Organic Colour Systems connection
- About / Services / Booking navigation

### Fix next

1. Make the main boxes and image areas feel more in proportion.
2. Make the service pathways easier to choose from.
3. Keep visible copy short, with Read More for detail.
4. Make phone and iPad layouts feel designed, not squeezed.
5. Add trust/social proof in the right places.
6. Make every button go to the correct section or booking route.

---

## 14. Practical checklist before approving any section

Before we approve a section, ask:

- Is it easy to understand in 5 seconds?
- Is there one clear main message?
- Is the image in proportion to the copy?
- Is the spacing calm and luxury?
- Is there too much visible text?
- Does the button take the client to the right next step?
- Does it work on phone and iPad?
- Does it feel organic, editorial and high-end?

---

## 15. Best working method from now on

We should not keep redesigning randomly.

For each section:

1. diagnose what is wrong
2. fix one issue
3. send visual proof
4. Sanchia approves or corrects
5. move to the next issue

This matches the video’s advice: strategy first, then design.
