# Homepage draft — Meet Sanchia

## Meet Sanchia

With more than 30 years of experience in hairdressing, I have learnt that truly beautiful hair begins with understanding the person in the chair.

Every appointment starts with listening. I take time to understand your hair history, natural texture, condition, lifestyle, daily routine and long-term goals, so we can create a hair journey that feels realistic, personal and completely tailored to you.

Whether you are visiting for colour, balayage, babylights, grey blending, textured cutting, natural curls, modern perming, beach waves, scalp care or a complete hair transformation, my aim is always the same — to help your hair feel healthier, easier to manage and more like you.

I do not believe hair should be treated as a one-off appointment. Your colour, cut, texture, treatments and homecare should work together over time, supporting the health, movement and integrity of your hair while creating a look that feels effortless, wearable and authentically yours.

For curly, wavy or textured hair, I take a gentle and specialist approach to curl health, hydration, definition and movement, helping your natural texture perform at its best in a way that suits your everyday life.

I also believe beautiful hair starts with healthy foundations. Through detailed consultation, scalp and hair assessment, carefully chosen treatments and professional technology such as Micro Mist, I can support the condition, comfort and long-term health of your hair and scalp.

The products I choose are just as important to me as the techniques I use. I care deeply about using professional products that support beautiful results while reflecting more conscious, cruelty-free and vegan-friendly choices wherever possible.

When I’m not behind the chair, you’ll often find me with my Ragdoll cat, Miss Evie, who has become a much-loved part of the Sanchia Hair story. Animals have taught me kindness, patience and respect for the natural world — values that quietly shape the way I care for every guest who sits in my chair.

Most of all, I want every guest to feel comfortable, listened to and genuinely cared for. Your hair journey should feel clear, collaborative and attainable — never overwhelming.

Your bespoke hair journey begins here.

Sanchia xx
