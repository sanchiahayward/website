About Sanchia


## Considered hair, guided by experience.

With more than thirty years in the industry, Sanchia brings deep technical experience, a calm one-to-one approach and a genuine understanding of how hair changes over time.

Every session is tailored to your individual needs. Sanchia takes time to understand your hair history, natural texture, scalp comfort, condition, lifestyle, routine and long-term goals before designing your colour, texture, cut, QiQi or Head Spa treatment journey — with the aim of creating a signature look that feels uniquely yours.

The benefit of working this way is that your hair is not treated as a one-off appointment. It becomes a considered journey: planned carefully, recorded with notes and photos where useful, and adjusted over time so the result continues to support your hair’s health, shape, movement and manageability.

As an Organic Colour Systems colourist, Sanchia has chosen to work with colour that aligns with her health-led, condition-conscious approach — supporting beautiful results while respecting the hair and scalp as much as possible.

Sanchia stays close to modern techniques and innovative professional products, always choosing what will genuinely support your hair, your lifestyle and the result you want — while protecting the integrity of the hair.

Her creativity is also shaped by a dynamic love of art, nature, music, design, interiors, sustainable fashion, upcycling, healthy food and thoughtful alternatives — using what already exists beautifully, reducing waste where possible and seeing potential in colour, texture, balance and personal style.

She also has a very strong passion for animals, which connects naturally to her cruelty-free values and the way she thinks about responsible product choices.

Her values are personal too. Working closely with professional products over many years has made Sanchia thoughtful about the choices she makes in salon — from hair and scalp comfort to her own wellbeing, the atmosphere she creates and the way each client feels cared for.

### Root Smudge & Toner

Add-on colour services that soften the root area, refine tone and create a more seamless finish.

**Read more**

Root smudge and toner services are usually add-ons to colour, highlights or balayage, and are recommended during consultation where they will improve the final result.

A root smudge gently blends your natural root colour into lighter lengths, softening harsh lines and helping the colour grow out more seamlessly. It can make highlights or balayage feel softer, more expensive and more natural-looking.

A toner is used after lightening or colouring to refine, balance and perfect the final shade. It can help neutralise unwanted warmth or brassiness, add softness, enhance brightness, create depth or make the colour feel more polished.

Together, they help extend the life of highlights or balayage and create a softer flow between light, depth and your natural colour.

Head Spa & Micro Mist Treatments


## Understand the difference before you book.

There are two different treatment routes on this page: the full Japanese or Korean Head Spa experience, and the shorter Micro Mist treatment menu. Both support scalp and hair health, but they are booked differently and include different levels of service.

Japanese and Korean Head Spa appointments are the complete rituals, with more time, more treatment layers and a deeper scalp-and-hair experience. Micro Mist treatments are shorter, focused treatment boosts at the backwash in the main salon. They can be booked on their own or added to selected services where suitable, but they are not the same as a full Head Spa appointment.
