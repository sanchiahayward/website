# Sanchia Hair Website Re-Organisation Plan

## Purpose
Use the existing approved template and the strong existing copy.

We are **not rewriting the copy** and **not changing the template direction**.

We are reorganising the website so it becomes:

- honest
- transparent
- clear
- easy to navigate
- luxury
- organic
- editorial
- calm
- informative but not overwhelming
- easy to read on phone and iPad

---

## Core client feeling

When a client lands on the website, they should feel:

- calm
- safe
- relaxed
- able to trust Sanchia
- able to ask questions
- that the service is personal and expert-led
- that this is a relationship, not just a one-off appointment

The website should attract clients who:

- value quality
- are willing to spend for expertise
- want a tailor-made experience
- want a haircut/service designed around their lifestyle
- understand hair is a journey
- are likely to rebook
- appreciate healthy, organic, animal-conscious and nature-led values

---

## Rule from the video

Every section must answer:

> Does this help the right client take the right next step?

If a section does not help the client understand, trust, choose or book, it needs to be simplified or moved.

---

## Font rule

Use a maximum of **three font styles** across the website.

### Font roles

1. **Editorial heading font**  
   Used for main headings and luxury magazine feeling.

2. **Clean readable body font**  
   Used for paragraphs, service explanations and practical information.

3. **Small accent / label style**  
   Used for small labels such as “About”, “Services”, “7am City appointments”, “Read More”.

### Important

Do not add extra random type styles.

No more than three visual font treatments.

---

## Spacing rule

Luxury needs breathing room.

Every section should have:

- enough space around headings
- enough space around images
- no cramped boxes
- no tiny text
- no overcrowded navigation
- images in proportion to copy
- readable line length

If a section feels squashed, reduce visible information before reducing spacing.

---

## Navigation structure

Keep top navigation simple:

1. About
2. Services
3. Booking

This keeps the top area clean and high-end.

Detailed service pathways should sit inside the Services section, not all across the top bar.

---

## Recommended website order

### 1. Top black 7am bar

Purpose: make early City appointments stand out immediately.

Needs to feel clear and visible.

Should communicate:

- 7am City appointments
- near Bank
- calm private-feeling hair care before work

---

### 2. Header / navigation

Purpose: help clients move without overwhelm.

Keep only:

- About
- Services
- Booking

No long row of service links at the top.

---

### 3. Hero section

Purpose: first impression and emotional positioning.

Keep:

- luxury organic editorial feeling
- slideshow/image box
- Curated Hair Journeys
- Organic Colour Systems connection
- one strong booking button
- one softer WhatsApp/message button

Needs to be checked for:

- image proportion
- heading size
- spacing
- button clarity
- phone/iPad readability

---

### 4. Trust strip / social proof near the top

Purpose: build confidence early.

This should appear before clients get too far down the page.

Could include short proof points such as:

- 30+ years experience
- Stā Studios near Bank
- Organic Colour Systems
- Google/Solo reviews
- specialist colour, curls, perms, QiQi and Hair Spa

Keep this elegant, not crowded.

---

### 5. About Sanchia

Purpose: trust, relationship, values and expertise.

Keep the existing strong copy.

Reorganise visually so the client quickly understands:

- Sanchia is experienced
- the experience is tailor-made
- hair is treated as a journey
- clients can speak openly
- the service is personal and calm
- values include animals, nature, health and responsible beauty

Use Read More if the section becomes too text-heavy.

---

### 6. Services pathway section

Purpose: stop clients feeling lost.

This section should act like a client guide, not a huge menu.

Use short pathway cards:

- Colour
- Smoothing / QiQi
- Perms / texture
- Hair Spa / Micro Mist
- Curls
- Consultation / not sure what to book

Each card should have:

- short title
- short helpful line
- Read More or link to deeper section

Avoid long visible text inside every card.

---

### 7. Colour section

Purpose: explain colour safely and clearly.

Keep existing copy.

Improve hierarchy:

- short intro first
- safety/testing clearly explained
- Organic Colour Systems trust point
- reviews or colour proof nearby
- Read More for details

---

### 8. Texture / curls / modern perms section

Purpose: explain specialist texture work clearly.

Needs to show:

- curls
- curl cutting
- modern perming
- spot perming
- lifestyle suitability
- tailored approach

Modern Perms links must go here.

Add relevant review/social proof where possible.

---

### 9. Smoothing / QiQi section

Purpose: explain QiQi as smoothing/control support.

Smoothing links must go here.

Needs to make clear:

- this is not old-style smoothing
- it can support frizz, bulk, shine, manageability and softer texture
- it is tailored to the client

Use strong image and simple visible copy.

---

### 10. Hair Spa / Micro Mist section

Purpose: make Hair Spa easy to understand.

Hair Spa links must go here.

Needs to show:

- Japanese/Korean Hair Spa
- Micro Mist
- scalp and hair health
- calm treatment experience
- difference between standalone Hair Spa and add-on treatment support

---

### 11. Private Studio / experience section

Purpose: explain atmosphere, privacy and emotional experience.

This should support:

- calm appointment experience
- morning appointments
- private-feeling care
- trust and relationship-building

---

### 12. Client care / policies

Purpose: honesty and transparency.

This section should be clear but not scary.

Use:

- short visible summaries
- Read More for detailed policies
- transparent wording around deposits, testing, lateness and adjustments

---

### 13. Reviews / recommendations

Purpose: strong social proof.

Reviews should appear in two ways:

1. Small review moments throughout the website.
2. A stronger review section lower down.

Review section should include:

- client recommendations
- Google/Solo reviews
- service-specific reviews where possible

Important: do not hide all reviews at the bottom only.

---

### 14. Gallery

Purpose: proof through real work.

Gallery should support the service pathways:

- colour
- grey blending
- curls
- perms
- smoothing/QiQi
- Hair Spa / experience

Do not let the gallery become overwhelming.

---

### 15. Booking / Visit

Purpose: make the final step easy.

Booking section should clearly show:

- how to book
- WhatsApp route
- consultation route
- location near Bank
- working hours
- what to do if unsure

This should feel calm and reassuring.

---

## Social proof placement

Social proof should be placed throughout the site.

### Suggested placement

- near hero: one short trust strip
- About: one personal trust/review quote
- Services: one review about feeling guided
- Colour: colour/highlight/grey blending review
- Texture/Perms: curl or perm review
- QiQi/Smoothing: smoothing/manageability review
- Hair Spa: calm/treatment review
- Booking: general trust/rebooking review
- Reviews section: full collection

---

## Read More rule

Keep visible copy short.

Use Read More for:

- detailed service explanations
- policies
- colour safety
- perming suitability
- QiQi details
- Hair Spa differences
- larger transformations

This keeps the website informative but not overwhelming.

---

## Button rule

Every button must go to the right place.

Current priority links:

- About → About section
- Services → Services pathway section
- Booking → Visit / booking section
- Smoothing → QiQi section
- Perms → Texture / modern perming section
- Hair Spa → Japanese Hair Spa / Micro Mist section

---

## Approval checklist for every section

Before approving any section, check:

- Is it easy to understand in 5 seconds?
- Is there one clear main message?
- Are fonts limited to the three chosen styles?
- Is the spacing calm?
- Is the image in proportion to the text?
- Is the copy easy to read?
- Is there too much visible text?
- Is there a clear next step?
- Does it work on phone and iPad?
- Does it feel luxury, organic, high-end and editorial?

---

## Working order from here

Do not rebuild everything at once.

Work in this order:

1. Top bar and navigation
2. Hero section proportions
3. Trust/social proof strip
4. About section proportions
5. Services pathway layout
6. Service sections one by one
7. Reviews/social proof placement
8. Booking/Visit section
9. Phone and iPad check
10. Final Squarespace implementation

---

## Key reminder

The copy is strong.

The template direction is right.

The work now is to make the website easier to use by improving:

- hierarchy
- spacing
- proportion
- social proof placement
- navigation
- Read More structure
- mobile/iPad readability
