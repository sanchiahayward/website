# Sanchia Hair — Service Reference Notes v1

Purpose: this is a working reference for revising copy across the whole website, not just the homepage. Use it as a copy/structure bank for service pages, private studio pages, and treatment pathways.

## Key direction

- Homepage should stay warm, personal and not too long.
- Detailed expertise belongs across service pages.
- Tone should feel like Sanchia speaking directly to the guest: expert, warm, attainable, calm, not corporate.
- Keep the right keywords but avoid making pages feel heavy.
- Use short intro paragraphs, clear service explanations, reassuring notes, and one strong quote/line per page.

## Japanese Head Spa & Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy

Working position: this should probably become a stronger dedicated area of the website rather than a small treatment block.

Suggested structure:

1. Japanese Head Spa & Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy
   - Beautiful hair begins with a healthy scalp.
   - Explain scalp/hair consultation, scalp analysis, porosity, elasticity, lifestyle, concerns and goals.
   - Cover dryness, oiliness, sensitivity, build-up, hair loss concerns, dehydration, colour damage and general scalp/hair health.

2. The Sanchia Hair Ritual
   - personalised scalp analysis
   - relaxing scalp massage
   - Organic Colour Systems botanical treatments
   - plant oils, amino acids, rosemary, nettle, oat, herbal extracts
   - exfoliating scalp treatment, scalp therapy, mask or intensive moisture treatment
   - personalised aromatherapy oils if confirmed

3. Advanced Micro-Mist Technology
   - ultra-fine nano vapour, not just ordinary steam
   - helps moisture/treatment ingredients penetrate more effectively into scalp and hair
   - heat/mist phase supports absorption
   - cooling mist helps smooth/close cuticle and seal in moisture and shine
   - improved hydration, softness, manageability and healthier-looking hair

4. Benefits
   - hydrates dry/dehydrated hair
   - supports scalp comfort and balance
   - enhances treatment performance
   - supports strength and elasticity
   - improves shine, softness, manageability
   - helps reduce dryness and frizz
   - encourages good conditions for healthy hair growth
   - creates a relaxing spa-like experience

Strong quote options:

> Beautiful hair begins with a healthy scalp. Everything else grows from there.

> Healthy hair doesn’t begin with the hair itself. It begins at the scalp, through thoughtful care, personalised treatments and creating the right environment for hair to thrive.

## Private Studio Appointments / Private Studio Experiences

Recommendation: use “Private Studio” rather than “Private Room.” It feels more luxury, intentional and aligned with the one-to-one bespoke brand.

Do not over-focus on religious reasons. Keep it inclusive:

- additional privacy
- quieter environment
- cultural or religious considerations
- anxiety or sensory sensitivities
- hair loss, thinning or scalp concerns
- medical hair journeys or recovery
- discretion/confidentiality
- guests who simply prefer uninterrupted one-to-one time

Suggested page/section structure:

1. Private Studio Appointments
   - Available by request for selected services.
   - Calm, comfortable, private and personalised.
   - Helps guests relax and focus on their wellbeing and hair journey without distraction.

2. Included Private Studio Experiences
   - Included with signature Japanese Head Spa and Korean Head Spa experiences.
   - These may be 2–3 hour immersive scalp, hair and wellbeing journeys once final timing is confirmed.
   - Include consultation, analysis, treatment, relaxation and aftercare recommendations.

3. Treatment elements to confirm
   - advanced scalp analysis / diagnostic technology
   - LED light therapy
   - micro-mist infusion
   - Organic Colour Systems botanical treatments
   - aromatherapy
   - exfoliation
   - massage techniques/tools
   - customised scalp and hair therapies

4. Personalise Your Experience add-ons
   - Blow-dry finish
   - Haircut & blow-dry
   - Curly cut & styling
   - Signature styling finish

5. Private Studio Investment
   - Private Studio appointments outside included Head Spa experiences available on request and subject to availability.
   - Additional charges based on time reserved and services being performed.
   - Personalised quotation provided before booking.

Strong quote:

> True luxury is not simply about the service you receive. It’s about feeling comfortable, understood and completely at ease throughout your experience.

Alternative quote:

> Hair health is a journey, not a quick fix. Sometimes the greatest luxury is giving yourself the time to slow down, reset and truly care for the foundation from which beautiful hair grows.

## Important copy rules

- Use “guests” rather than “clients” where natural.
- Use “available by request” and “subject to availability” for private studio.
- Use “personalised quotation” for private studio pricing.
- Leave timing/pricing placeholders until confirmed.
- Avoid overclaiming medical or hair-loss results. Use “supports”, “helps create optimal conditions”, “healthy-looking hair”, “scalp comfort”, “hair growth concerns” rather than promising regrowth.
- If mentioning LED, aromatherapy, diagnostic technology, Korean Head Spa, or specific treatment inclusions, confirm they are definitely part of the service before publishing.
