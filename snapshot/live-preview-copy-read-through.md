## About Sanchia

Considered hair, guided by experience.

With more than thirty years in the industry, Sanchia creates curated hair journeys built around intention, honesty and the way you live.

Every session is tailored to your individual needs. Sanchia works one-to-one, taking time to understand your hair history, natural texture, condition, lifestyle, routine and long-term goals before designing your colour, texture, cut or treatment journey — with the aim of creating a signature look that feels uniquely yours.

The process is thoughtful and personal: respecting your hair’s growth, integrity and structure, then creating a bespoke plan that supports enduring transformation without compromise — especially for early 7am City guests who want their hair finished before the working day begins.
