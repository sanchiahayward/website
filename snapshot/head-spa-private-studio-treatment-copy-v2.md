## Which Head Spa Is Right For You?

While both treatments focus on scalp health, healthy hair growth and overall wellbeing, they offer two different experiences.

### Japanese Head Spa

The Japanese Head Spa is centred around relaxation, restoration and holistic wellbeing.

Often described as a facial for the scalp, this treatment combines scalp analysis, therapeutic massage, aromatherapy, botanical treatments, Micro-Mist therapy and sensory rituals designed to calm the nervous system while improving scalp and hair health.

The focus is not only on achieving healthier hair, but also creating a moment of pause in an increasingly busy world.

This treatment may be ideal if you:

- want to reduce stress and tension,
- enjoy massage and relaxation therapies,
- experience scalp dryness or sensitivity,
- want to improve overall hair and scalp health,
- are looking for a luxurious self-care experience,
- value preventative scalp and hair wellness.

### Korean Head Spa

The Korean Head Spa takes a more advanced, treatment-focused approach.

Inspired by Korea’s skincare philosophy, the scalp is treated with the same level of analysis, precision and personalised care that would normally be given to facial skin.

Using advanced scalp analysis, targeted exfoliation, detoxification therapies, red light therapy, Micro-Mist technology and customised scalp treatments, the focus is on creating the healthiest possible environment for scalp function, hair growth support and long-term hair wellbeing.

This treatment may be ideal if you:

- experience excess oil or scalp congestion,
- have dandruff, itching or scalp imbalance,
- are concerned about thinning hair or hair loss,
- want a deeper scalp detox,
- enjoy results-driven treatments,
- are looking for intensive scalp rejuvenation.

## Important Health & Allergy Information

Your health, safety and wellbeing are extremely important.

Please inform us at the time of booking if you have any allergies, sensitivities, medical conditions or concerns that may affect your treatment.

Due to the use of aromatherapy oils, botanical extracts, essential oils, scalp treatments, massage techniques, heat therapies and specialised treatment products, Japanese and Korean Head Spa experiences may not be suitable for everyone.

Please notify us before booking if you:

- are pregnant or breastfeeding,
- have allergies or sensitivities to aromatherapy oils,
- have allergies to nuts, nut oils or nut-derived ingredients,
- have allergies to plants, flowers, herbs, botanical extracts or natural ingredients,
- have asthma, respiratory sensitivities or fragrance sensitivities,
- have scalp infections, open wounds, abrasions, psoriasis flare-ups or severe scalp conditions,
- have recently undergone scalp surgery or medical procedures,
- have any medical condition that may be affected by heat, massage or aromatherapy treatments,
- are currently undergoing medical treatment that may affect the scalp or hair.

As every treatment is customised, we encourage you to share any concerns before your appointment. This allows us to determine suitability and create the safest and most enjoyable experience possible.

If you are unsure whether these treatments are suitable for you, please contact us before booking and we will be happy to advise.

> Personalised care begins with understanding. The more we know about your hair, scalp and wellbeing, the better we can tailor your experience to support you safely and beautifully.

## Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy

At the heart of the treatment is Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy.

Unlike traditional steamers, Micro-Mist technology creates an ultra-fine vapour that allows moisture and treatment ingredients to penetrate more effectively into both the scalp and hair.

The warm mist gently opens the hair cuticle and scalp surface, allowing professional treatments, scalp oils, treatment sprays and conditioning masks to travel deeper into the areas where they are needed most.

This helps to:

- improve moisture retention,
- increase treatment absorption,
- support scalp health,
- improve softness and manageability,
- reduce dryness and frizz,
- enhance shine and elasticity,
- support healthier-looking hair.

Once the treatment has fully penetrated, a cooling phase helps smooth and close the cuticle, sealing in moisture and shine for longer-lasting results.
