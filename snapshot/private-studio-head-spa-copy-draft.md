# Private Studio Appointments

For guests who would like a quieter, more personal experience, Private Studio Appointments are available by request.

Some guests enjoy the gentle energy of the salon, while others feel more comfortable in a calm one-to-one space where they can fully relax, speak openly and enjoy their appointment with added privacy.

A Private Studio Appointment may be helpful if you prefer a more discreet experience, have cultural or personal privacy needs, experience anxiety or sensory sensitivity, are experiencing hair loss or scalp concerns, are on a medical hair journey, or simply want uninterrupted time to focus on your hair and wellbeing.

The aim is always to help you feel comfortable, understood and completely at ease throughout your visit.

> True luxury is not simply about the service you receive. It is about feeling comfortable, understood and completely at ease throughout your experience.

## Services available privately

All Sanchia Hair services can be requested as a Private Studio Appointment, subject to availability.

This may include:

- colour and grey blending,
- highlights, balayage and babylights,
- cutting and styling,
- natural curl cutting and styling,
- modern perming and texture services,
- smoothing and texture transformation,
- Japanese Head Spa,
- Korean Head Spa,

Private Studio access is included with selected signature Head Spa experiences. For other services, the private studio can be requested as an add-on where available.

As every appointment is tailored to the individual, any additional charge is based on the time reserved in the Private Studio and the services being performed. A personalised quotation will be provided before confirming your appointment.

Please contact us before booking if you would like to arrange a Private Studio Appointment.

# Japanese Head Spa & Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy

Beautiful hair begins with a healthy scalp.

My Japanese Head Spa and Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy treatments are designed to restore balance to the scalp, deeply nourish the hair and create a moment of calm in an increasingly busy world.

Each treatment begins with a detailed scalp and hair consultation. I assess your scalp condition, hair health, porosity, elasticity, lifestyle, concerns and long-term goals before creating a treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Whether you are experiencing dryness, excess oil, sensitivity, product build-up, hair loss concerns, dehydration, colour damage or simply want to improve the overall condition of your hair and scalp, every treatment is carefully customised to support your individual hair journey.

## The Sanchia Hair ritual

Your experience begins with a personalised scalp analysis, followed by a relaxing scalp massage designed to release tension, support circulation and encourage a healthier scalp environment.

Using carefully selected professional treatments, your scalp and hair are treated according to what they need on the day. Depending on your consultation, this may include scalp exfoliation, moisture therapy, treatment masks, scalp balancing care, intensive repair, aromatherapy and therapeutic massage techniques.

The aim is not to follow one set routine for every guest. The aim is to create a calm, restorative treatment experience designed around your scalp, your hair and the way you want to feel.

## Advanced Micro-Mist technology

At the heart of the treatment is Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy.

Unlike a standard steam treatment, Micro-Mist creates an ultra-fine mist that helps professional treatments absorb more effectively into the hair and scalp. This supports hydration, softness, shine, manageability and scalp comfort.

The warmth of the mist helps open the hair cuticle so moisture and treatment ingredients can reach the areas where they are needed most. A cooling phase can then help smooth the cuticle, helping to seal in moisture and leave the hair feeling softer, smoother and more conditioned.

## Benefits of Micro-Mist Therapy

Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy may help to:

- deeply hydrate dry or dehydrated hair,
- improve softness, shine and manageability,
- support scalp comfort and balance,
- enhance the performance of professional treatments,
- support strength and elasticity,
- reduce the feeling of dryness and frizz,
- create a calmer, spa-like treatment experience,
- support a healthier environment for long-term hair and scalp wellbeing.

## More than a treatment

This is more than a hair treatment.

It is a ritual designed to slow you down, restore balance and support the long-term health of your hair and scalp.

By combining consultation, advanced treatment technology, carefully chosen products and therapeutic touch, each session becomes a bespoke wellness experience for both your hair and your wellbeing.

> Hair health is a journey, not a quick fix. Sometimes the greatest luxury is giving yourself the time to slow down, reset and truly care for the foundation from which beautiful hair grows.
