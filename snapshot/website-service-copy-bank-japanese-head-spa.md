# Refined website copy draft

## Japanese Head Spa & Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy

Beautiful hair begins with a healthy scalp.

My Japanese Head Spa and Advanced Micro-Mist Therapy treatments are designed to restore comfort and balance to the scalp, deeply nourish the hair and create a moment of calm in an increasingly busy world.

Each treatment begins with a detailed scalp and hair consultation. I take time to understand your scalp condition, hair health, porosity, elasticity, lifestyle, concerns and long-term goals before creating a bespoke treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Whether your hair or scalp is feeling dry, sensitive, oily, congested, dehydrated, affected by colour or simply in need of deeper care, every treatment is carefully customised to support your individual hair journey.

## The Sanchia Hair Ritual

Your experience begins with a personalised scalp analysis, followed by a relaxing scalp massage designed to release tension, encourage circulation and support a healthier scalp environment.

Using carefully selected professional botanical treatments, your scalp and hair are treated with ingredients chosen for your individual needs. Depending on your consultation, this may include nourishing plant oils, amino acids, rosemary, nettle, oat and herbal extracts, alongside targeted scalp and hair treatments.

A customised exfoliating scalp treatment, treatment mask, scalp therapy or intensive moisture treatment may then be applied depending on what your hair and scalp need on the day.

To make the experience feel even more personal, aromatherapy oils can be selected according to your needs, helping to create a deeply relaxing sensory treatment.

## Advanced Micro-Mist Technology

At the heart of the treatment is the Advanced Micro-Mist system.

Unlike a simple steam treatment, Micro-Mist creates an ultra-fine mist that helps professional treatments work more effectively on the hair and scalp. The gentle warmth and moisture help soften and open the hair cuticle so carefully selected treatments can absorb more effectively where they are needed.

After the treatment phase, a cooling mist helps smooth the cuticle, supporting softness, shine, moisture retention and a more conditioned feel.

The result is hair that feels softer, calmer, more manageable and beautifully cared for, with the scalp feeling refreshed and supported.

## Benefits of Micro-Mist Therapy

Micro-Mist therapy can help to:

- deeply hydrate dry or dehydrated hair
- improve scalp comfort and balance
- enhance the effectiveness of professional treatments
- support hair strength and elasticity
- improve shine, softness and manageability
- reduce the feeling of dryness and frizz
- support a healthier scalp environment
- create a deeply relaxing spa-like experience

## More Than A Treatment

This is more than a hair treatment.

It is a ritual designed to slow you down, restore balance and support the long-term health of your hair and scalp.

By combining advanced technology, botanical ingredients, personalised consultation and therapeutic touch, each session becomes a bespoke wellness experience for both your hair and your wellbeing.

Just as a beautiful garden begins with healthy soil, beautiful hair begins with a healthy scalp. Everything else grows from there.
