# About

About Sanchia

## Considered hair, guided by experience.

With more than thirty years in the industry, Sanchia brings deep technical experience, a calm one-to-one approach and a genuine understanding of how hair changes over time.

Every session is tailored to your individual needs. Sanchia takes time to understand your hair history, natural texture, condition, lifestyle, routine and long-term goals before designing your colour, texture, cut or treatment journey — with the aim of creating a signature look that feels uniquely yours.

The benefit of working this way is that your hair is not treated as a one-off appointment. It becomes a considered journey: planned carefully, recorded with notes and photos where useful, and adjusted over time so the result continues to support your hair’s health, shine, shape, movement and manageability.

As an Organic Colour Systems colourist, Sanchia has chosen to work with colour that aligns with her health-led, condition-conscious approach — supporting beautiful results while respecting the hair and scalp as much as possible.

Sanchia stays close to modern techniques and innovative professional products, always choosing what will genuinely support your hair, your lifestyle and the result you want — while protecting the integrity of the hair.

Her creativity is also shaped by a dynamic love of art, nature, music, design, interiors, sustainable fashion, upcycling, healthy food and thoughtful alternatives — using what already exists beautifully, reducing waste where possible and seeing potential in colour, texture, balance and personal style.

She also has a very strong passion for animals, which connects naturally to her cruelty-free values and the way she thinks about responsible product choices.

Her values are personal too. Working closely with professional products over many years has made Sanchia thoughtful about the choices she makes in salon — from hair and scalp comfort to her own wellbeing, the atmosphere she creates and the way each client feels cared for.

# Colour

Colour safety

## Bespoke colour with safety built in.

For colour, balayage, highlights, grey blending and colour corrections, the journey should feel expert and reassuring — not rushed.

New colour guests may need a consultation, photos, hair history and a valid skin test before colour is confirmed.

An in-person consultation can include skin testing or hair strand testing where suitable. Guests can also request a posted skin-test kit after completing the form and pre-paying, if appropriate. Prices and booking options are kept on Solo so they stay current.

Sanchia has chosen to work with Organic Colour Systems because their values sit closely with her own: professional colour created by hairdressers, for hairdressers, with a more thoughtful approach to hair health, product responsibility and salon sustainability. Their heritage dates back to 1990 and their work continues to focus on colour performance, ethical standards and kinder choices for salons and their clients.

This product story supports the way Sanchia works: choosing colour systems and treatment rituals that help achieve beautiful, bespoke results while respecting the hair, scalp and long-term condition as much as possible.

Within the colour journey, selected products can include conditioning colour bases, bespoke shade options, protective lightening support and plant-derived conditioning ingredients such as guar gum, chosen to support shine, softness, manageability and moisture.

Sanchia believes in being transparent about the products used in salon. During consultation, she can explain why a product or system has been chosen, what it is designed to support and how it fits into your wider hair plan, so the process feels honest, informed and supportive.

If skin-test requirements are not completed in time, your colour appointment may need to be rescheduled.

# Texture

Natural curls / textured perming

## Rejuvenating Natural Curls Treatment and modern textured perming.

From supporting the curls you already have to creating soft, modern movement through textured perming, Sanchia’s approach is bespoke, realistic and condition-led. Results can last up to six months, depending on hair type, condition, homecare and maintenance.

01
### Rejuvenating Natural Curls Treatment

This is for guests who already have natural wave, curl or movement but feel their hair has become dry, frizzy, stretched, uneven or difficult to style. The treatment journey can support hydration, shape, definition, softness and confidence in wearing your natural texture. Results can last up to six months, depending on the hair and aftercare.

02
### Modern textured perming

This is for guests who want to create soft beach-wave texture or a more lived-in curl effect. The consultation looks at condition, previous colour, hair history, lifestyle and maintenance before deciding what type of textured result is suitable.

**Benefits:** modern textured perming can add natural-looking movement, support flat or lifeless hair, create body at the root, make styling feel easier, help a blow dry last longer and give the hair a softer, more effortless shape without needing to curl it every day.

**The difference:** this is not one fixed result. It may mean enhancing naturally looking curls, creating beautiful beach waves, or working only on selected areas where you would like a little more movement, lift or volume. The technique is chosen around your hair, your lifestyle and the finish you want to live with.

Treatment pathway

### Why it benefits natural curls

Natural curls often need moisture, structure and the right product support to sit well. The Rejuvenating Natural Curls Treatment is designed to revive curl memory, improve softness, support definition and reduce frizz, with results that can last up to six months depending on the hair, aftercare and home routine.

For guests who want a different level of curl control, Sanchia can also discuss curl-loosening or smoother-finish treatments to soften tightness, reduce frizz and make wash-and-wear styling easier, or spot perming to restore curl pattern, add root lift or rebalance areas where the curl has dropped.

Products / Hair Integrity

### Chosen to support the hair, not compromise it.

Every product used within Sanchia Hair is selected with the condition, strength and long-term feel of the hair in mind. For curl and texture services, Sanchia can explain the professional systems used in salon and why they have been chosen for a more considered approach to modern perming.

The aim is always to be transparent and honest: explaining the value of the products, the role of homecare and the realistic maintenance needed, so clients feel supported rather than sold to.

Organic Colour Systems Think Curl can be introduced here in a client-friendly way: a modern texture system enriched with plant amino acids, designed to help create beautifully defined movement while supporting the hair’s internal bonds.

Curl Cutting & Styling

### A longer appointment, shaped around each curl.

This appointment begins with a personalised consultation, carefully assessing your curl pattern, natural growth pattern, hair history, density, condition, lifestyle and how you prefer to style your hair at home.

Sanchia then shapes the hair in its dry, natural state, working with the way each curl falls so the cut respects movement, balance and individuality. After cleansing and treating the hair to support condition, the curls are styled and the shape is refined with freehand detailing — subtly carving, slicing or adjusting where needed to perfect the silhouette.

Each step honours the movement and individuality of your curls while helping the shape feel wearable, considered and easy to live with. Product recommendations are tailored to your curl type, routine and desired finish, so your hair can continue to sit well after you leave the salon.

Perm confidence / testing

### Try the curl shape before committing.

For perming and texture work, Sanchia can carry out strand testing where suitable. If you are unsure about the curl shape, a small hidden test section can be permed in a place that can be checked visually but is not obvious day to day.

This allows you to see the shape, live with it for a little while and feel confident before committing to a fuller perm or texture service.

Homecare / aftercare support

### Leave with the right products and guidance.

For first-time perm, rejuvenating curl, smoothing or major transformation guests, Sanchia can build a tailored homecare pack into the plan so the right products are ready to take home.

This helps create a smooth transition from salon to home, especially when a guest is using a new service for the first time. The pack can include products chosen for their hair needs, plus a clear do’s-and-don’ts information card so the first wash, styling routine and ongoing care feel simple and supported.

For new texture services, the first 72 hours are especially important. Guidance may include when to wash, how to style gently, avoiding tight ponytails or pulling the hair back, and avoiding strenuous brushing or tools while the new curl pattern settles.

Homecare is recommended according to your hair’s needs, wet stretch test results and styling routine, so the result can last beautifully and feel manageable at home. If you feel unsure once you are home, Sanchia can offer WhatsApp support or a short video call to guide you through the routine again, helping you feel confident and fall in love with your new look.

Planning ahead

### Pre-booking keeps your hair journey on track.

To help protect your results and keep your plan moving, Sanchia recommends pre-booking your next appointment before you leave. Depending on the service, maintenance may be recommended around 6, 8 or 12 weeks — guided by what you are having done, your hair goals and the look you want to achieve.

If you need a particular day, time or 7am appointment, you can also ask to be added to the waiting list.

Gifts & Homecare

## Gift cards, products and guest love.

A Sanchia Hair gift card is a thoughtful way to give time, care and a luxury hair experience — while allowing the person receiving it to choose what feels right for them.

The gift card can be created to the value the giver would like to spend. That value can then be used towards a hair service, styling care, a treatment ritual, professional homecare, or a considered mix of both.

For example, the recipient may choose to use part of the value towards an appointment and part towards take-home care products chosen to suit their hair needs, within the amount gifted.

Gift cards are presented as a printed card in a small black envelope. They can be collected from the salon, or posted directly for an additional postage fee.

To keep the gift discreet and elegant, the value is not written on the card. Each gift card can include a private scannable code so Sanchia can check the value when it is redeemed, without the recipient seeing the amount spent.

Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. They are non-refundable, cannot be exchanged for cash and any unused balance expires after the valid-until date.

They are especially lovely for someone who would value calm one-to-one time, a treatment ritual, a confidence refresh or a before-work 7am City appointment that fits around a busy life.
