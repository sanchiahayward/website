# Services overview

## Clear pathways, not confusing menus

The website should help you understand what direction may be right for you before you book. Solo holds the exact appointment options, timings and prices, but the website is here to explain the difference between services so you feel confident choosing the right route.

Prices are from. Final cost depends on hair length, density, condition, colour history, product use and time required. Larger changes may need a consultation, strand test, skin test, staged plan, homecare support or more than one appointment.

## Consultation

For new guests, colour changes, texture work, smoothing, curls, corrective work or anyone unsure what to book.

A consultation is where we look at your hair properly: what has been done before, what is realistic, what your hair can safely manage, what products may be needed, and what route gives you the best chance of achieving a beautiful result without compromising the condition.

## Cutting & Styling

Haircuts, blow dries, shaping and finishing designed around how your hair naturally sits.

Your cut is planned around texture, growth pattern, density, face shape, lifestyle and how much time you actually want to spend styling your hair at home.

## Curl Cutting & Styling

For guests who want their natural curls, waves or texture to feel understood.

The hair is assessed in its natural state so the shape works with the way the curl falls in real life. After cleansing, treatment and styling, the shape can be refined so the finish feels balanced, wearable and easy to live with.

## Highlights

Highlights are for guests who want brightness, dimension, face-framing lightness or a naturally blended lift through the hair.

The placement is planned around your haircut, face shape, natural texture, growth pattern and maintenance level, so the colour works with the way your hair moves rather than sitting separately from the cut.

## Balayage

Balayage is different from traditional highlights. The lightness is painted and placed more freely to create a softer, lived-in result with a more natural grow-out.

It is ideal if you want brightness and movement without a hard starting point, or if you prefer a lower-maintenance colour journey that still feels polished and bespoke.

## Root Smudge & Toner

A root smudge softens the transition between your natural root and lighter lengths. A toner refines warmth, brightness, depth or softness after lightening.

These are small details that make colour feel expensive and seamless.

## Permanent, Semi & Demi Colour

Permanent colour is used when the aim is longer-lasting colour change or stronger grey coverage.

Semi-permanent colour is softer and lower commitment. It can refresh tone, add shine or gently enhance what is already there.

Demi-permanent colour sits between the two. It is useful for gloss, depth, tonal change and softer grey blending with a more gradual fade.

Sanchia will choose the most suitable option during consultation, based on your hair history, condition, grey level and desired result.

## Grey Blending

Grey blending is not about automatically covering every grey. It is about deciding how much grey you want to soften, blend or keep visible.

Highlights, lowlights, toners and thoughtful placement can create a softer, more modern result that works with your natural silver and is often easier to maintain.

## Modern Perming

Modern perming is not about making hair look obviously “permed.” It can be used to create beach-wave movement, root volume, selected texture, curl support or a more natural-looking shape.

The service is planned around your hair condition, colour history, lifestyle and the finish you want to live with. Strand testing may be recommended where needed.

## QiQi Hair Controller

QiQi is the flexible hair-control treatment Sanchia now offers.

QiQi Hair Controller has replaced older smoothing services because it offers newer, more flexible technology that can be personalised around your natural texture and your goals.

Some guests want smoother curls. Some want softer waves. Some want less frizz, less bulk, easier styling, more shine or a sleeker finish. The aim is not to remove your hair’s personality. It is to help your hair feel healthier, more manageable and easier to wear every day.

With the correct homecare, results can typically last around four to six months, depending on hair type, condition, lifestyle, aftercare and how often the hair is washed or heat-styled.

# Natural curls / texture / perming

## Natural texture should be designed, not forced

Sanchia’s curl and texture work is about understanding how the hair behaves naturally, then deciding what will support it best.

For some guests, that means a dry curl cut and treatment to revive softness and definition. For others, it may mean modern perming to create beach-wave movement, root lift, selected curl support or a more lived-in shape.

# Micro Mist treatments

## Shorter treatments. Still deeply beneficial.

Micro Mist treatments are shorter hair and scalp treatment services performed at the backwash in the main salon. They can be added to selected appointments such as colour, cutting or perming, or booked as a shorter treatment where suitable.

Each treatment begins with a hair and scalp assessment. Where appropriate, a wet hair test can help guide which products will give the best support on the day.

Using Organic Colour Systems botanical shampoos, treatment sprays and intensive masks — with specialist scalp scrubs or nourishing oils where suitable — Sanchia tailors the treatment to your hair and scalp needs.

The treatment is enhanced with Micro Mist technology. Open-air vapour helps prepare the hair, then the Micro Mist cap uses gentle heat and moisture to support treatment absorption.

Each guest receives a complimentary disposable heated eye mask to make the experience feel more relaxing.

## Micro Mist menu

Hydrate — 15 minutes. For dry or thirsty hair needing moisture, softness and shine.

Repair — 15 minutes. For damaged or chemically treated hair needing strength and support.

Scalp Balance — 15 minutes. For scalp refresh, comfort and balance using products selected for your needs.

Detox & Moisture — 20 minutes. A more intensive Micro Mist option combining scalp detoxification with deep hydration for both scalp and hair.
